# Proofline Product Logic

Proofline is not a cheaper NiceJob clone. It is an AI growth operator for local businesses.

The core promise:

> Every completed customer interaction should become one of five outcomes: public review, recovered customer, reusable proof asset, referral lead, or repeat booking.

## Core Loop

1. Capture a real business event.
   - Completed job
   - Paid invoice
   - Appointment finished
   - CSV import
   - Review received
   - Customer reply
   - Call or form lead

2. Normalize the customer state.
   - Consent status
   - Last contact
   - Service type
   - Sentiment
   - Revenue value
   - Open issue
   - Review history

3. Pick the safest next action.
   - Ask for a review
   - Send a follow-up
   - Draft a public reply
   - Create a recovery task
   - Publish proof to widget/social/site
   - Ask for referral
   - Send repeat reminder
   - Suggest gift or service credit
   - Hold for owner approval

4. Execute or queue.
   - Low-risk actions can run automatically.
   - Customer-facing sensitive actions require approval.
   - Compliance blockers always stop automation.

5. Measure result.
   - Review completed
   - Reply received
   - Issue resolved
   - Quote approved
   - Referral lead created
   - Repeat booked
   - Gift sent
   - Revenue influenced

## Why This Is Different

Most reputation tools sell modules. Proofline sells the daily owner decision:

> What should I do today to get more reviews, save unhappy customers, and turn happy customers into revenue?

That single queue is the product center. Review requests, AI replies, referrals, repeats, gifts, insights, sites, integrations, and compliance all feed that queue.

## Automation Levels

Level 1: Assist
- Draft replies
- Suggest review request text
- Summarize feedback

Level 2: Operate
- Send eligible requests
- Stop follow-ups on reply or opt-out
- Update widgets
- Generate weekly brief

Level 3: Decide with guardrails
- Classify recovery risk
- Recommend gift or referral
- Prioritize owner queue

Level 4: Managed growth
- Done-for-you setup
- Monthly optimization
- Microsite updates
- Campaign tuning
